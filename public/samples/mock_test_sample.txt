[SECTION: QUANT]

Directions (Q1-Q2): The following data shows the number of accounts (Savings and Current) opened in five different branches (A, B, C, D, E) of a public sector bank:
- Branch A: 250 Savings, 150 Current
- Branch B: 350 Savings, 200 Current
- Branch C: 300 Savings, 320 Current
- Branch D: 420 Savings, 400 Current
- Branch E: 500 Savings, 280 Current
Study the data carefully and answer questions 1 and 2:
[IMAGE: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1551288049-bebda4e38f71?w=800]

Q1. What is the ratio of total savings accounts opened in branches A and B together to the total current accounts opened in branches C and D together?
(A) 4 : 5
(B) 5 : 6
(C) 7 : 8
(D) 3 : 4
(E) 8 : 9
Ans: (B)
Marks: +1.0, -0.25
Explanation: Total savings in A and B = 250 + 350 = 600. Total current in C and D = 320 + 400 = 720. Required ratio = 600 : 720 = 5 : 6.

Q2. The total number of accounts opened in Branch E (500) is what percentage more or less than the total accounts opened in Branch A (400)?
(A) 15% more
(B) 20% less
(C) 25% more
(D) 30% more
(E) 10% less
Ans: (C)
Marks: +1.0, -0.25
Explanation: Difference = ((500 - 400) / 400) * 100 = 25% more.

[STANDALONE]
Q3. Find the missing number in the given series: 12, 26, 54, 110, 222, ?
(A) 442
(B) 444
(C) 446
(D) 448
(E) 450
Ans: (C)
Marks: +1.0, -0.25
Explanation: Pattern: *2 + 2. 12*2 + 2 = 26; 26*2 + 2 = 54; 54*2 + 2 = 110; 110*2 + 2 = 222; 222*2 + 2 = 446.

Directions (Q4-Q5): Eight professionals — P, Q, R, S, T, U, V, and W — are sitting in a straight row facing North, but not necessarily in the same order:
1. P sits third from one of the extreme ends.
2. Only two persons sit between P and T.
3. V sits immediate left of T.
4. W sits at the extreme left end of the row.
5. Arrangement from left to right: W - R - P - V - T - U - S - Q.

Q4. Who sits at the extreme left end of the row?
(A) S
(B) W
(C) R
(D) U
(E) Q
Ans: (B)
Marks: +1.0, -0.25
Explanation: Based on clue 4, W sits at the extreme left end.

Q5. How many persons sit between P and W?
(A) One
(B) Two
(C) Three
(D) Four
(E) None
Ans: (A)
Marks: +1.0, -0.25
Explanation: Only R sits between P and W.

Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs) are legal tender issued by a central bank in digital form. The Reserve Bank of India has piloted the Digital Rupee (e-Rupee) for wholesale and retail segments to reduce the physical cost of managing currency and foster financial inclusion.

Q6. According to the passage, which of the following is a primary objective of launching the Digital Rupee (e-Rupee)?
(A) To eliminate traditional bank branches completely
(B) To lower the operational expenses of cash management and enhance financial inclusion

(E) To mandate crypto mining across public sector institutions
Ans: (B)
Marks: +1.0, -0.25
Explanation: The passage directly mentions reducing physical cash logistics costs and fostering financial inclusion as key goals.

(C) Dismantle
(D) Ignore
