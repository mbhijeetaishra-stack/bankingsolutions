BSCA Daily Current Affairs & GA Quiz
Date: 2026-10-25

Q1. Which regulatory body in India recently introduced the comprehensive regulatory framework on digital lending and unauthorized loan apps?
(A) Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)
(B) Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
(C) Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority (IRDAI)
(D) Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA)
(E) National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD)
Ans: (B)
Explanation: The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) issued regulatory guidelines for digital lending to protect borrowers from predatory lending practices, unauthorized apps, and unethical recovery tactics.

Q2. Who among the following has been appointed as the Executive Director for India at the International Monetary Fund (IMF)?
(A) Raghuram Rajan
(B) Viral Acharya
(C) Urjit Patel
(D) K.V. Subramanian
(E) Arvind Subramanian
Ans: (D)
Explanation: Former Chief Economic Adviser K.V. Subramanian was appointed as the Executive Director (India) at the International Monetary Fund (IMF).

Q3. What is the minimum Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) / Capital to Risk (Weighted) Assets Ratio (CRAR) required to be maintained by Scheduled Commercial Banks in India as per RBI Basel III norms?
(A) 8.0%
(B) 9.0%
(C) 10.5%
(D) 11.5%
(E) 12.0%
Ans: (B)
Explanation: As per RBI's Basel III guidelines, Scheduled Commercial Banks in India are required to maintain a minimum Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) of 9.0% (excluding Capital Conservation Buffer).

Q4. In which state was India's first international multi-modal logistics park developed under the Bharatmala Pariyojana?
(A) Gujarat
(B) Maharashtra
(C) Assam
(D) Tamil Nadu
(E) Karnataka
Ans: (C)
Explanation: India's first international multi-modal logistics park (MMLP) was developed at Jogighopa in Assam under the flagship Bharatmala Pariyojana.

Q5. What is the current maximum transaction limit per day for Small Value Digital Payments in Offline Mode (UPI Lite) as revised by the Reserve Bank of India?
(A) Rs. 1,000
(B) Rs. 2,000
(C) Rs. 5,000
(D) Rs. 10,000
(E) Rs. 20,000
Ans: (B)
Explanation: The overall limit for small value digital transactions in offline mode on a payment instrument is Rs. 2,000 at any point in time, with individual transaction cap of Rs. 500.

Q8. In the Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) of the Reserve Bank of India, how many members are there in total?
(A) 4
(B) 5
(C) 6
(D) 7
(E) 8
Ans: (C)
Explanation: The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) consists of 6 members: 3 from the Reserve Bank of India (including the RBI Governor who acts as the ex-officio chairperson) and 3 external members appointed by the Central Government.

Q9. What is the statutory lock-in period for investments made in the Public Provident Fund (PPF) scheme in India?
(A) 5 Years
(B) 10 Years
(C) 15 Years
(D) 20 Years
(E) 25 Years
Ans: (C)
Explanation: The Public Provident Fund (PPF) account has an initial statutory maturity period of 15 years, which can be extended in blocks of 5 years thereafter.

Q10. Where is the permanent headquarters of the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB) located?
(A) Manila, Philippines
(B) Beijing, China
(C) Shanghai, China
(D) Tokyo, Japan
(E) Singapore
Ans: (B)
Explanation: The Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB) is a multilateral development bank headquartered in Beijing, China, of which India is the second-largest shareholder.
